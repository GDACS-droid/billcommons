# Bill Commons data terms

_Also published at `/terms`. See `NOTICE` for the underlying software
license (Apache-2.0) and full source attribution list; this document
covers the terms of the paid/keyed *service* on top of that data._

## No copyright claim over public records

Bill Commons does not claim copyright in the underlying legislative bill
text, actions, sponsorships, or votes. Those are, in general, public-domain
works of state and federal government bodies in the United States (17
U.S.C. § 105 for federal works; most states apply an equivalent
public-domain treatment to legislative and statutory text). What you pay
for on a paid plan or a snapshot purchase is **access, normalization, and
delivery** — a maintained API, a documented schema, a working search index,
and a bulk export you don't have to build yourself. Attribution and
no-resale below are *contract terms of the service*, not a copyright claim
over the facts.

## Attribution (all tiers, including free/anonymous use)

Attribute Bill Commons as the source wherever you display or publish data
obtained from this API or a snapshot:

> Data via Bill Commons (billcommons.org)

Where the underlying record originated with Open States (Plural Policy),
their own attribution obligation passes through — see
[openstates.org](https://openstates.org) and `NOTICE` for the full source
list. Don't imply the underlying government body endorses your product.

## Snapshot files: no resale, derived works fine

Snapshot files (the one-time state/full-corpus Parquet exports, and the
nightly snapshots included with a Scale subscription) **may not be
redistributed or resold as-is**. Building a product, analysis, or report
on top of the data is fine and encouraged — attribute it. Redistribution
rights (reselling the raw files, or a close derivative of them, to third
parties) are available on an Enterprise agreement; email
sales@billcommons.org.

## API keys: no sharing across unrelated organizations

An API key is issued to one customer (one email/organization). Don't share
a key across unrelated companies or resell access to your key's quota.
Unusually broad sharing (many distinct networks hitting one key in a day)
gets flagged for a conversation, not an automatic block — see the
`/docs/api-keys` page and the operations runbook.

## Refunds

**Subscriptions (Builder, Scale):** full refund within 7 days of the
charge; prorated after that. Contact sales@billcommons.org or use the
"Manage billing" button on `/account`, which opens the Stripe customer
portal.

**Snapshots (one-time state/full-corpus purchase):** refundable **until
the download link is sent** — once the file has been delivered, the
purchase is final. This is the practical dividing line for a digital good
that can't be "returned": say so plainly, here and on `/docs/bulk`, above
the buy button, so nobody is surprised.

## 90-day price grandfathering

Anyone who becomes a paying customer within the first 90 days of Builder
or Scale going on sale keeps their price for as long as their subscription
stays active and in good standing, even if list prices change later.

## Anonymous use

Anonymous (keyless) API access stays free, subject to a per-IP/per-network
daily request cap (see `/docs/api-keys` for the current numbers) — this is
not a paywall on ordinary search or reasonable API use, it's an abuse
ceiling.
